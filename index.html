    Route Builder

    Compose cheap model first routing with confidence based escalation, then replay the policy against a labeled synthetic request log to see the cost and quality tradeoffs.

    Policy Settings

    Synthetic Requests come from a seeded synthetic log with difficulty labels.

    A request first tries its routed model. While confidence sits below the threshold it retries one tier up, and every attempt is billed.

    Routing Rules

    First matching rule wins. A blank escalation threshold means never escalate.

    Replay

    Difficulty is the minimum fixture tier the label says a request needs. Success means the final model tier meets it. Confidence values are a synthetic proxy, not measured scores.
